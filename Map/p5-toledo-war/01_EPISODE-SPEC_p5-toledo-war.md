## SCENE 1 — Hook
- **VO:** "This peninsula touches Wisconsin. It belongs to Michigan. Blame Toledo."

## SCENE 2 — The 1787 line
- **VO:** "In 1787, Congress drew a line straight east from the bottom of Lake Michigan. Michigan above, Ohio below. One problem: the maps of the day put the lake too far north."

## SCENE 3 — Two surveys, one strip
- **VO:** "Ohio's survey put the line above the mouth of the Maumee River, and the town of Toledo. Michigan's survey followed the 1787 law, and kept Toledo. Between them: a strip up to eight miles wide."

## SCENE 4 — The war
- **VO:** "By 1835, both sides were ready for war. Michigan's governor was 23. The Boy Governor. Ohio set aside $300,000 for the fight. Michigan set aside $315,000. Its militia fired into the air and arrested nine surveyors. So who got hurt? One sheriff. He was stabbed with a penknife, by a man named Two Stickney."

## SCENE 5 — The deal
- **VO:** "Then Congress made an offer: give Ohio the strip, take the western Upper Peninsula instead. Michigan said no. Then, in a freezing December, it said yes. Some say the Ohio State–Michigan rivalry started right here."

## SCENE 6 — The consolation prize
- **VO:** "And the consolation prize? Iron. And copper. In 1844, a surveyor named William Burt watched his compass needle go haywire. The ground under him was full of iron. Then, for forty years, Michigan mined more copper than any other state."

## SCENE 7 — Wrap + signature
- **VO:** "Ohio got Toledo. Michigan got the copper. The headline changes. The map doesn't."
